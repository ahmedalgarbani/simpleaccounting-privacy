Simple Accountant is your personal money book: record debts (what others owe you and what you owe them), track income and expenses, and get accurate accounting reports — fully offline, with your data stored only on your device.

📒 Debt book
• People with opening balance and phone number.
• "For him / On him" entries with an instant net balance per person.
• Transfer an amount between two accounts in one step.
• Fast search and filters: owes, owed, settled.
• Remind people via WhatsApp or SMS using your own message template.
• Add a debt entry by voice.

💰 Income & expenses
• Categorised income and expenses (housing, food, transport, health…).
• Monthly summary and easy month navigation.
• Monthly spending limit with alerts.

📊 Real accounting reports
• Every transaction is a balanced double-entry journal entry.
• Financial position, income statement and trial balance.
• Per-person account statement exported to PDF or Excel and shared in one tap.

💱 Multiple currencies with exchange rates.

🔒 Privacy & security
• Works completely offline; no personal data is collected.
• PIN app lock.
• Backup and restore at any time.

🎨 Comfortable design
• Arabic and English, light and dark mode, accent colour and font size options.
• Phones and tablets.
